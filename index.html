About

“A place is worth visiting when a single frame can tell you why.”

Heritage sites and cultural landmarks, through the lens

La Frames India is a small, growing catalogue of the country's most photographed places — built for travellers who want the practical details (how far, from where) alongside the reason to go.

  • Every entry is chosen for what it looks like in person, not just in a photograph.
  • Airport distances are listed first, because logistics decide itineraries.
  • New cities are added as the collection grows.
